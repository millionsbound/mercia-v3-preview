How we vet the specialists we introduce

We only introduce firms that have passed the checks below. We repeat them every year, and sooner if a certificate expires or a customer tells us something has gone wrong.

The 8 verification checks

CheckHow we check itCan you check it too?
The business exists and is who it says it isCompanies House for limited companies; trading address and owner's photographic ID for sole traders and partnerships.Yes. Free search on find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk.
Waste carrier registrationThe Environment Agency public register (environment.data.gov.uk). Any firm removing waste must hold an active Upper Tier registration.Yes. We provide you with their registration number and register search link before introduction.
Public liability insuranceA copy of the current insurance certificate, with policy coverage confirmed and expiry date recorded.Yes. You can inspect their certificate alongside your written quote.
Employer's liability insuranceA copy of the statutory employer's liability certificate if the firm employs staff.Yes. Available upon request from the contractor.
Electrical work competencyRegistration with a competent person scheme such as NICEIC or NAPIT, checked on the scheme's public register.Yes. Both NICEIC and NAPIT host public online registers.
Experience with this kind of jobPhotos of previous jobs, with addresses and faces removed, and a clear description of their strip-out method.Yes. Ask the specialist to show past examples of their insurer-ready handover packs.
Customer referencesAt least two recent customers, or a verifiable track record of genuine public reviews.Yes. Specialists can provide references upon request.
Staff checksWe ask whether staff working in occupied or vulnerable settings are DBS checked.Yes. You can verify staff checking policies directly.

The waste carrier number, before we introduce anyone

We will give you each firm\'s waste carrier registration number and the official Environment Agency register link before making an introduction. You can check the register directly before agreeing to speak with them.

Note: We do not display static registration numbers on our website as partner registrations are actively verified per job and renew annually.

After every introduction: 1-week and 1-month follow-up

After every introduction, we follow up with you a week and a month later to ask whether the work went ahead and how it went.

If a firm does not respond quickly, does poor work, or treats a customer badly, we stop introducing them immediately.

What we cannot promise

Checks reduce risk; they do not remove it. You contract with the specialist directly, so read their terms, get a written quote and scope, and do not pay large sums up front.

If something goes wrong, tell us. See our complaints procedure.
