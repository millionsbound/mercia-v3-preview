What happens during the clearance in Solihull

1. Initial callback: We verify the property postcode and discuss whether West Midlands Police have released the scene and provided your Crime Reference Number.

2. Physical survey: An introduced contractor who operates across Solihull inspects the building. They measure moisture levels in timber, note drilled floorboards, calculate waste volume, and check electrical cut-out safety.

3. Controlled strip-out: Contaminated soil, plastic pots, high-intensity discharge lamps, transformers, and ducting are bagged and loaded under an Environment Agency Waste Carrier licence.

4. Odour & mould eradication: Biocidal treatments kill black mould spores trapped behind foil sheeting, followed by ozone or thermal fogging to neutralise skunk odours.

5. Insurer documentation: You receive official Waste Transfer Notes, before-and-after photographic records, and itemised invoices suitable for insurance loss adjusters and tenancy deposit adjudications.
