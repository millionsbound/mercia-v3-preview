Complaints Procedure

Last updated: March 2026

How to complain

If you have a concern regarding our introduction service or an introduced firm, please use our request form or call Hamza Yusuf directly on 07498 603180.

Tell us what happened, the property postcode, and, if the issue relates to a specialist contractor, which firm you were dealing with.

What we will do

  • We will acknowledge your complaint within 2 working days.
  • We will look into the matter thoroughly, including asking the specialist for their side if the complaint concerns their conduct or quotation.
  • We will give you a full written response within 14 days, or explain clearly why it will take longer.

Complaints about a specialist\'s work

Because your contract is directly with the specialist contractor, they are legally and contractually responsible for putting any defect or workmanship issue right.

However, we take all customer feedback seriously. We will raise the issue directly with contractor management, and if we are not satisfied with how they respond or treat you, we will immediately stop introducing them to landlords.

Many professional trade schemes, such as NICEIC and NAPIT for electrical work, or the Environment Agency for waste handling, operate their own formal consumer complaints processes.

Data protection complaints

For any concerns regarding personal data handling, please refer to our Privacy Policy. You also hold the right to contact the Information Commissioner\'s Office (ICO).
