About Mercia Property Recovery

We are an introduction service, not a cleaning or building company. We do not carry out the work ourselves. We connect landlords and letting agents in the West Midlands with specialists we have checked, and we may receive a fee from those specialists.

Why this service exists

When police find a cannabis farm in a rented property, they remove the plants and move on. Search for help and you mostly find news reports and police appeals. Landlords are left working out, on their own and in a hurry, who to call about the electrics, the waste, the damp, the insurer and the tenant.

This site tries to fix that in two ways: plain guides that explain what to do next, and a single place to ask for help from people who do this work.

How we are paid

You do not pay us. The specialists we introduce may pay us a fee for each introduction, and in some cases a further fee if the job goes ahead.

This does not change the price you are quoted, and you are free to get other quotes. We will always tell you, before your details are passed on, which firm or firms will contact you.

What we do and do not do

What we do

  • ✓We listen to what happened, ask the questions a specialist will need answered, and introduce you to up to 2 vetted specialists who cover your postcode.
  • ✓We follow up afterwards to check the work went ahead and was done properly.
