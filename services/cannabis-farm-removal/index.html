What the police remove, and what they leave behind

When a residential rental or commercial building is raided, officers from the West Midlands Police Cannabis Disposal Team enter the property to secure the scene, preserve forensic evidence, and neutralise the illicit cultivation. According to published operational procedures from West Midlands Police Cannabis Disposal, their statutory remit is strictly focused on dismantling the farm and seizing evidence. Officers cut down the plants, confiscate mature buds and seedlings, seize chemical nutrients and weapons, and bag key ballasts and electronic timers required for court prosecution.

What police do not do is clear the house or repair the building. Once the investigation concludes and the property is formally handed back to the landlord or letting agent, you are responsible for clearing the aftermath:

  • Hundreds of kilograms of soaked compost and root balls: Plants are cut at stem level, leaving hundreds of plastic pots filled with heavy damp soil that quickly turns stagnant.
  • Pervasive reflective sheeting and framing: Walls and ceilings are routinely lined with heavy silver Mylar foil or black-and-white plastic sheeting, fixed with industrial spray glue and batten screws that damage plaster.
  • Suspended ventilation and air filtration equipment: Heavy commercial carbon filters, flexible aluminium ducting, and noisy inline centrifugal fans left mounted to structural joists.
  • High-voltage domestic modifications: Bypassed consumer units, looped cables, cut ceiling roses, and scorched sockets where circuits were overloaded for months.
  • High-humidity mould and chemical odours: Moisture trapped behind foil lining creates black mould (Stachybotrys) on internal walls, while volatile cannabis terpenes penetrate deep into bare brickwork and plaster.

Cannabis factory removal: what the job covers

Cannabis factory removal is not standard domestic rubbish removal. It is a controlled environmental strip-out requiring specialised personal protective equipment, waste classification, and sanitisation. In tenancy deposit dispute records published by the mydeposits scheme, a landlord was awarded £250 on an invoice dedicated solely to clearing pots, compost and sheeting from a small grow room. For larger multi-room properties, the scale and complexity multiply substantially.

A professional strip-out systematically clears every room, disassembles timber grow tents, takes down high-intensity discharge (HID) lights, packages hazardous fertilisers, treats surfaces against fungal spores, and consigns all waste through authorized treatment centres.

How cannabis farm removal works: the five-stage process

1) Call-back and the basics

When you submit your enquiry, our team reviews the property location and situation. We call you back to confirm whether police have released the keys, whether power has been safely isolated at the street cut-out, and the approximate size of the property (terraced house, semi-detached, flat or commercial unit).

2) Survey and photos before anything moves

A vetted local specialist visits the address to conduct a physical survey. Crucially, no price is agreed before this inspection. The specialist inspects waste volumes, moisture readings in plaster, ceiling duct cut-outs, and access restrictions (such as narrow shared terraced alleyways common in Birmingham and the Black Country). Comprehensive photographs are logged before any item is touched, establishing proof for your insurance company and tenancy deposit adjudicators.

3) Strip-out and licensed waste removal with transfer notes

Technicians equipped with respirators and protective coveralls bag all contaminated compost, root balls, nutrients, and sheeting. Everything is loaded directly into licensed commercial vehicles or enclosed lockable skips. If a firm cannot provide an active Upper Tier Environment Agency Waste Carrier registration number, do not use them. You receive an official stamped Waste Transfer Note verifying lawful disposal.

4) Make the electrics safe

Criminal syndicates almost universally bypass electric meters by hacking into unmetered incoming mains cables. In one published Yardley case reported by Birmingham Live, the landlord faced nearly £5,000 in costs, including nearly £3,000 charged by the distribution network operator just to replace the tampered service head. A certified NICEIC or NAPIT electrician removes unsafe domestic wiring and isolates circuits safely.

5) Decontamination, odour treatment and a handover pack

Masking sprays do not work against cannabis cultivation residue. Specialists apply biocidal washdowns to eradicate toxic mould, followed by industrial ozone generation or thermal fogging to neutralise terpene molecules embedded in plaster. You are handed a comprehensive pack containing before-and-after photographs, waste transfer manifests, and itemised invoices.

What cannabis farm removal does not cover

It is important to understand the boundary between removal and building restoration. Removal focuses strictly on strip-out, waste hauling, and odour decontamination. It does not include:

  • Street service head replacement: Only National Grid Electricity Distribution (call 105) can reconnect tampered underground mains cables.
  • Full electrical rewiring: Reinstalling circuits, sockets and new metal consumer units falls under our dedicated Electrical Make-Safe & Rewire service.
  • Structural carpentry and replastering: Reinstating cut ceiling joists, hanging fire doors, and skimming damaged walls falls under our Property Restoration service.

How to check a waste carrier licence

Under the Environmental Protection Act 1990 and the Waste (England and Wales) Regulations 2011, landlords have an absolute statutory duty of care. Handing waste to an unlicensed carrier who subsequently fly-tips it on a country lane leaves you legally liable for criminal prosecution and unlimited fines.

Before any firm collects waste, verify their registration on the Environment Agency Public Register. Check that their registration status is "Upper Tier Carrier Dealer" and that the business name matches the entity carrying out the work. When Mercia introduces a specialist, we provide their registration number and register link directly to you.

Flats, HMOs and empty commercial units

Grow operations in multi-unit premises present specific hazards:

  • Flats and shared blocks: Shared riser cupboards and false ceilings are frequently compromised, allowing water leaks or heat to penetrate neighbouring leasehold properties. Coordinated waste staging is essential so communal stairwells remain clear.
  • Licensed HMOs: High heat and bypassed consumer units directly breach mandatory HMO fire compartmentation conditions. Restoring fire doors, self-closers, and fire-stopping seals is required by local councils before occupancy resumes.
  • Disused commercial units: Industrial units often involve high-voltage three-phase installations, massive extraction fans, and hazardous asbestos roof sheets that must not be disturbed during ducting removal.

Before you book: essential landlord protocols

  1. Wait for police release: Do not enter or permit any contractor onto the premises until West Midlands Police have concluded investigations and given formal clearance.
  2. Do not switch the electricity on: Bypassed cut-outs mean live conductors can energise gas pipes, radiators or damp surfaces.
  3. Call 105: Notify National Grid Electricity Distribution of tampered incoming cables immediately.
  4. Photograph every room: Capture dated visual proof of all equipment, cut joists, and moisture levels before any waste is disturbed.

Cannabis farm removal FAQs

Can I clear a cannabis farm myself to save money?

We strongly advise against self-clearance. Untrained landlords risk electrocution from tampered wiring, lung infections from mould spores, and hefty legal fines if waste is transported without an Environment Agency carrier licence.

How quickly can a specialist attend the property?

Attendance depends on local specialist availability and whether police have released the scene. We aim to call you back the same working day, and surveyors typically attend within 24 to 48 hours of release.

Will the contractor deal with police on my behalf?

No. The contractor cannot represent you in criminal investigations. You must obtain the Crime Reference Number and release details yourself. However, the contractor supplies comprehensive paperwork for your insurer and deposit scheme.

Do I have to pay Mercia Property Recovery?

No. Our service is completely free to landlords and letting agents. You pay the contractor directly according to their agreed quote.
