Frequently asked questions

Can the specialist provide the evidence for my deposit claim?

They can provide photos, a written scope and itemised invoices. The check-in and check-out reports still need to come from you or your inventory clerk.

Do you handle hoarded properties?

Yes. Introduced teams clear heavy hoards, biohazards, and cluttered waste safely. Tell us about the situation when we call.

Can I throw away possessions left behind by the tenant immediately?

You cannot simply bin them straight away. Write to the former tenant at their last known address and any forwarding address, give them a reasonable deadline to collect, and keep a record. Take legal advice if the items look valuable.
