Electrical make-safe, testing and rewire

Electricity theft is a feature of most cannabis farms. The meter may be bypassed, cables may be run straight from the supply, and new circuits may have been added with no protection. This is the most dangerous part of the property. Nobody should switch anything back on until it has been checked.

Key capabilities:

  • ✓Coordination with National Grid Electricity Distribution (call 105) regarding incoming service cut-outs
  • ✓Safe isolation of tampered circuits and removal of grow-room wiring, timers and extra sockets
  • ✓Inspection and thorough testing by a registered electrician, producing an EICR listing defects
  • ✓Carrying out remedial electrical work, consumer unit replacement, and necessary rewiring

Drug den and needle clean-up

When a property has been used for drug taking, the danger is often hidden: needles in sofas, under carpets, in loft insulation and behind bath panels. This is not a job for a general cleaner. We introduce you to biohazard specialists who do needle sweeps and deep cleans with the right equipment and disposal.

Key capabilities:

  • ✓Sharps sweeps of every room, including soft furnishings, under flooring, and inside loft spaces
  • ✓Containerisation of sharps in approved puncture-proof boxes taken for incineration as clinical waste
  • ✓Biohazard cleaning and disinfection of contaminated surfaces, bodily fluids, blood and heavy soiling
  • ✓Removal and lawful disposal of contaminated items such as mattresses, soft furniture and carpets

Squatter removal and property clearance

The right approach depends on the type of property and who the occupiers are. Get it wrong and you can end up committing an offence yourself. We help you work out which route applies, then introduce you to the right people: enforcement agents, solicitors, clearance firms and security.

Key capabilities:

  • ✓Guidance on the legal distinction between residential squatting (LASPO 2012 s144) and commercial occupation
  • ✓Introduction to certificated enforcement firms for commercial premises and land, or SRA-regulated solicitors where an Interim Possession Order (IPO) is needed
  • ✓Clearance of squatter waste by an Environment Agency registered waste carrier
  • ✓Biohazard or needle sweeps where properties have been occupied without sanitation
