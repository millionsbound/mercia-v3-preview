The first 48 hours: what landlords must do

Acting in the right order protects your insurance claim and prevents fatal electrical shock.

Read full 48-hour checklist →
Hour 0

Crime reference & release

Obtain the officer collar number and Crime Reference Number. Never enter the building until police have formally concluded their investigation and released custody.

Same Day

Leave power off & call 105

Do not touch the fuse box. In almost every grow, mains cables are spliced before the meter. National Grid Electricity Distribution must make the incoming supply safe.

Same Day

Notify insurer & request quote

Inform your insurer to satisfy strict notification windows. Send us your postcode so we can identify checked specialists who cover your borough.

Days 1 - 2

Photograph every room

Take comprehensive photographs before anything moves. Do not throw away soil, sheeting, or cut joists without clear photographic proof for adjudicators.

Days 2 - 7

Survey and written quote

The introduced specialist surveys the premises and gives you a fixed written quote with line items for waste transfer, odour treatment and rebuild.

Week 2 Onwards

Clear, dry out, rewire & certify

Controlled strip-out under EA licence, industrial drying, rewiring, and issuing an updated EICR before redecoration and re-letting.

Frequently asked questions

Plain facts on costs, police custody, waste liability and insurance.

How much does this service cost me as a landlord?

Mercia is completely free for landlords and letting agents. You do not pay us anything. You deal with the introduced specialist directly and pay them their agreed price. The specialist may pay us an agreed introduction fee, which is always openly disclosed.

How many contractors will contact me?

No more than two. We call you first to confirm the property postcode and requirements. We then name the specific firm or firms before passing any contact details across, so you never receive unsolicited calls from random tradesmen.

Can I get in touch before the police have formally released the property?

Yes. You can send us the postcode and details immediately so we can line up the survey. However, nobody can enter the building until investigating officers confirm that forensic work has concluded and the property has been returned to your possession.

What if my landlord insurer wants to appoint their own contractor?

If your insurer accepts the claim and insists on appointing their own managed contractor network, you should follow your insurer’s instructions. If your insurer asks you to provide independent quotations, our specialists provide itemised estimates formatted for loss adjusters.

Can the clearance team use unmarked vehicles?

Yes. Neighbours often notice police attendance, but to maintain discretion during subsequent strip-out works, you can request unmarked vans.

Do you also help with drug dens, squatters, or general tenant damage?

Yes. In addition to cannabis farms, we introduce specialist teams for hypodermic needle sweeps, biohazard deep cleans, squatter clearances and heavy tenant neglect across the West Midlands.

Speak to our team today. We verify what happened, check local specialist coverage, and guide you through the first safe steps.
